A LITTLE ABOUT US

Useful ideas for a wardrobe that feels like yours.

Rue Notes is an independent journal about personal style, everyday clothing care, and making thoughtful choices without making getting dressed complicated.

Why Rue Notes?

Our clothes are part of ordinary life: the walk to work, the school run, the dinner that runs late. We think style advice should make those days easier. Rue Notes shares practical guides and approachable ideas to help you get more use from the clothes you own and make considered choices when you do shop.

Our editorial approach

We aim to publish clear, original, and useful articles. We try to keep advice realistic, explain the reasoning behind it, and avoid presenting one body, budget, or lifestyle as the right one. Clothing-care guidance is general: always check the care label and manufacturer instructions for a specific garment.

We value accuracy and welcome corrections. If you spot something that needs attention, contact the site operator using the contact details published on this site.

Advertising and independence

This site may display advertisements provided by Google AdSense. Ads are marked as advertisements and are separate from editorial articles. The presence of an advertisement does not mean Rue Notes endorses a product or service. Any future sponsored article or affiliate relationship will be clearly disclosed alongside the relevant content.

Get in touch

We welcome thoughtful feedback, questions, and corrections. Before publishing, the site owner should add a real, monitored email address to this section.
