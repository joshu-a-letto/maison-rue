YOUR PRIVACY

A clear note about data and cookies.

This policy explains what may be collected when you visit Rue Notes and how advertising on this site works.

Last updated: October 10, 2026

Who operates this site?

Rue Notes is an independent editorial website. Before launch, the site owner should add their legal name and a real, monitored email address to the contact section on the About page so visitors can make privacy requests.

Information collected when you visit

Like most websites, the hosting provider may process technical information needed to deliver and protect the site, such as an IP address, browser and device details, requested pages, and request time. This static site does not provide user accounts, comments, or a newsletter sign-up form.

Advertising, cookies, and similar technologies

This site uses Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to collect or receive information from this site and elsewhere on the internet, including information about your browser or device, in order to provide, measure, and personalize advertisements where permitted by your settings and applicable law.

Learn more about how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. You can review or change Google's advertising personalization settings at Google My Ad Center. You can also manage cookies through your browser settings; disabling cookies may affect advertising or other site features.

Where required by law, a consent message may be needed before certain advertising technologies are used. The site owner is responsible for configuring a suitable consent solution for the regions and advertising settings in which this site operates.

Other third-party services

This site loads fonts from Google Fonts and editorial photographs from Unsplash. When your browser requests these resources, those providers may receive technical information such as your IP address and browser details. Their own privacy policies apply to those requests.

Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information, or to object to or limit certain processing. You can also manage browser cookies and advertising personalization using the controls described above. Contact the site operator using the real contact details published on this site to make a privacy request.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the site or its services change. The date at the top of this page indicates when it was most recently revised.
